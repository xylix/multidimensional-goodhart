# Spine: Measurement Dimensions and Hidden Harm

This is the compact source of truth for the next manuscript spine. Do not
structurally rewrite `book/multidimensional-goodhart.typ` until this framing is
approved.

## Central Question

How do measurement dimensions affect hidden harm?

## Core Answer

The main closed result is the additive exchange-rate iff-condition: in the
additive fixed-deficit model, changing measured dimensions conserves hidden
harm exactly when hidden loss is proportional to measured contribution,
`h_j = c w_j`, on the active channels. More generally, measurement dimensions
have no generic effect; they can conserve, reduce, increase, or reroute hidden
harm depending on aggregation, action costs, stakes, response channel, and the
declared harm or value primitive. The response-modeling contract is the larger
framework that licenses this result and explains why it does not transfer
without declared primitives.

## Contribution Stack

- **Primary formal contribution:** a conservation/rerouting diagnostic for
  measurement dimensions, centered on T5, the additive exchange-rate
  iff-condition.
- **Supporting methodological contribution:** a response-modeling contract that
  says which primitives must be declared before importing a Goodhart
  calculation.
- **Persistent guardrail:** score movement alone does not identify mechanism,
  hidden welfare, target improvement, or the selection/intervention split.
- **Comparison target:** slogan-level Goodhart claims, generic dimensionality
  claims, score-only interpretations, and unlicensed theorem transfer from one
  response model to another.
- **Calibration:** this is a closed formal toolkit plus methodological
  reframing, not a universal multidimensional Goodhart law and not a policy
  prescription.

## Refused Claims

- No generic claim that more measurement dimensions are better, worse, safer,
  or more harmful.
- No welfare conclusion without a declared value or harm primitive.
- No inference from score movement alone to hidden value, mechanism, response
  channel, or target improvement.
- No generic dimensionality theorem based on dimension count alone.
- No transfer of selection, intervention, adaptive-hardening, or
  response-shape results outside their declared primitives.
- No promotion of T1/T2/T3/T4/T6 to front-door status unless they directly
  serve the measurement-dimension question.

   measurements or making more dimensions of error measurable.
2. **Challenge:** show why observed score movement underidentifies hidden harm

3. **Failed generic laws:** use the killed claims to explain why there is no
   unconditional multidimensional Goodhart law.
4. **Proposal:** introduce the response-modeling contract as the primitive
   declaration needed before any calculation is licensed.
5. **Central diagnostic:** state T5 as the main theorem about conservation and
   rerouting under additive fixed-deficit measurement.
6. **Non-transfer boundary:** explain exactly why T5 requires its aggregation,
   cost, deficit, and harm assumptions.
7. **Supporting calculations:** include selection drift, intervention
   affordability, and adaptive hardening only where they clarify the central
   measurement-dimension question.
8. **Aggregation and entry:** separate fixed-deficit per-agent harm from
   population entry, changed aggregation rules, and welfare claims.
9. **Prior-work relation:** map nearby literatures by which primitives they
   supply and where transfer fails; frame the project as reduction, not
   unification.
10. **Stress tests:** use cases to demonstrate primitive declaration and
    refusal discipline, not to claim broad empirical policy results.
11. **Falsifiers and refusals:** state what would make the framework return no
    verdict and what would count against a licensed calculation.
12. **Open agenda:** keep broad the extensions beyond additive separable
    fixed-deficit models, including aggregation interactions,
    selection/intervention/population entry, and response geometries that might
    preserve clean conservation or rerouting claims.

## First-page Rule

The abstract and first page should be organized around the measurement-dimension
question, not around the contract in isolation. T5 should be visible before any
theorem inventory. The contract should then explain why the result is licensed,
why score movement alone is insufficient, and why other closed calculations are
supporting tools rather than competing headlines.
