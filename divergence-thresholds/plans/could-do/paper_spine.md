## Working title

*Divergence Thresholds: When Activated Goodhart Stays Bounded vs. Goes
Catastrophic*

## Core claim (one line)

The parent characterizes *whether* gaming activates; this paper characterizes
*whether activated gaming diverges*. The discriminating primitive is the tail
index of hidden value/harm relative to the optimization channel, not the
magnitude of optimization pressure. The threshold is conditional on declared
primitives — there is no universal scalar law.

## Why this is a separate paper, not a chapter

The parent manuscript's central caution is that there is no universal scalar
"Goodhart effect size"; measurement is licensed only after declaring the
response channel, hidden value/harm, aggregation, action geometry, and evidence
standard. A threshold/divergence narrative, if folded into that book, risks
reading as the universal law the book refuses. Held separately and stated
conditionally, it instead supplies the severity half that the parent's
activation thresholds lack.
