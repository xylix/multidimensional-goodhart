# Closed questions

Closed here means closed under the project's current claim license, not solved
in every possible model.

## Killed claims

1. Dimension count alone does not determine hidden harm.
   Owner: `research/negative_results.md`, Iteration 1.

2. Signed aggregate hidden error is not a stable welfare object.
   Owner: `research/negative_results.md`, Iteration 1.

3. Baseline covariance is not a general finite-pressure selection primitive.
   Owner: `research/negative_results.md`, Iterations 2-3.

4. "More measured dimensions helps" and "more measured dimensions hurts" have
   no sign without aggregation, exchange rates, population entry, and value
   weights.
   Owner: `research/negative_results.md`, Iterations 5-6.

5. Additive conservation is not generic. Fixed-deficit per-agent conservation
   in the quadratic additive toy holds exactly under `h_j = c w_j` on the
   active channels.
   Owner: `research/threads/core_propositions_and_value_norms.md`.

6. Absolute continuity is not the causal intervention boundary.
   Owner: `research/negative_results.md`; `research/threads/response_kernel_boundary.md`.

7. The response-kernel selection/intervention split is not representation-free
   and not identifiable from marginal laws alone.
   Owner: `research/negative_results.md`; `research/claim_audits.md`.

8. Convex affordability and the score-deficit cost `m(d)` are not welfare
   bounds without a declared hidden harm/value functional.
   Owner: `research/negative_results.md`; `research/threads/convex_intervention_bound.md`.

9. Generic minimum-complexity attraction fails. Selection follows baseline tail
   response; intervention follows cost/search geometry.
   Owner: `research/threads/minimum_complexity_attractor.md`.

## Narrow theorem-boundary closures

1. Deterministic finite-channel adaptive hardening is closed only inside the
   Iteration 35 contract: fixed measured set `M`, fixed deficit `d`, fixed
   stakes `V`, fixed weights, additive score gain, separable quadratic costs,
   deterministic observation, and monotone lowering of `kappa_j`. Gaming is
   feasible iff `S_t(M) >= d^2/(2V)` and stops exactly when
   `S_t(M) < d^2/(2V)`.

2. Iteration 36 closes only a dynamic-toy boundary: stochastic observation and
   endogenous-stakes rules can alter transfer from the static capacity story.
   It does not close fixed-point, filtering, persistent-cycle, or
   performative-stability questions.

3. Iteration 37 closes only an empirical-design boundary: application
   primitives can sometimes be predeclared, bounded, defended, or marked
   unavailable with design consequences. It does not estimate hidden targets.

4. Iteration 38 blocks broad policy prose. Licensed actions are narrow
   audit/design/evidence actions inside a declared application contract:
   document effective rules, audit repeated-unit action traces, monitor hidden
   target traces where available, lower leverage or pilot when primitives are
   unavailable, and withhold toy diagnostics when primitives are missing.
