# Parked questions

Parked questions are not active work until their missing primitive is fixed
before inspection and a failure condition is declared.

## P1. Recursive / minimum-complexity attractor

Question: as proxy pressure or proxy repair increases, when does hidden drift
converge toward a low-complexity configuration?

Why parked: the unconditional claim is killed. A revived version must choose a
complexity functional before looking at the outcome: support size, rank or
spectral concentration, description length, KL from a reference distribution,
semantic legibility, or another explicit measure.

Required before activation:
- response mechanism: quadratic cost, fixed charge, capped linear channels,
  low-rank action map, search prior, empirical candidate generator, or another
  declared geometry;
- representation in which complexity is measured;
- failure condition that can fire.

Current owners: `research/threads/minimum_complexity_attractor.md`;
`research/verification_stack.md`.

## P2. Lean validation

Question: should the theorem core be formalized in Lean?

Why parked: era 1 deliberately finishes the book, abstract, and
`research/core-math.md` first. Lean is an era-2 option, not an active era-1
task.

Activation gate: `research/core-math.md` exists, book proposition pointers are
stable after the spine and polish passes, and a human deliberately chooses the
Lean roadmap.

Current owner: `plans/could-do/lean_validation_roadmap.md`.
