# Direction and adaptive-frontier review synthesis

Status after consolidation: historical but still useful direction synthesis.
This file replaces the post-Iteration-25 direction memo and the Iteration 27
adaptive-hardening review. Use it for the research-direction lesson and the
finite-channel adaptive-frontier toy boundary. Do not use it as current
theorem, policy, manuscript, or integration authority.

## Consolidated verdict

The project made real progress by killing broad dimensional, covariance,
absolute-continuity, additive-conservation, and minimum-complexity slogans and
replacing them with scoped response-channel primitives. The surviving thesis is
that multidimensional Goodhart should be treated as a response-modeling
framework, not as a universal dimensional or complexity law.

The main residual risk is taxonomy-led survivability. A response contract is
valuable only when it forces a licensed calculation, a discriminating
observation, a changed decision, or a possible failure of the framework.

## Direction lesson

The Lakatos/Tao/Hossenfelder/Feynman review found that many repairs were real
lemma incorporation rather than slogan protection:

- covariance sufficiency was narrowed to linear conditional-mean settings;
- selection/intervention was moved from marginal absolute continuity to
  declared fixed-type kernels;
- additive conservation was narrowed to equal harm per score unit;
- minimum-complexity attraction was blocked unless a complexity functional and
  response mechanism are fixed in advance.

The review also warned that "declare the response model" can become too easy
to keep alive. Future applications and extensions need framework-level failure
conditions: cases where plausible evidence cannot distinguish response
contracts, where contracts fit the same observations but imply opposite design
advice, or where required primitives cannot be bounded before observing the
failure.

## Adaptive hardening toy boundary

Iteration 27 licenses a narrow Layer-3 toy result:

Under a declared finite-channel additive quadratic scorecard with fixed
`kappa`, `h`, `gamma`, `w`, fixed stakes `V`, and deterministic deficits,
reactive hardening changes the capacity

`S(M) = sum_{j in M} kappa_j w_j^2`.

In that contract, hardening can converge to no-gaming once
`S(M) < d^2/(2V)`. Active gaming routes can switch before convergence. Added
metrics can either expand attack surface or improve net benefit depending on
signal, harm, attack surface, and stakes. Static narrow commitment can
dominate broad reactive measurement when signal adequacy is predeclared.

## Non-license

The adaptive-frontier toy does not license theorem-level convergence for
arbitrary hardening policies, empirical claims about real metric systems,
endogenous-stakes dynamics, stochastic observation, policy optimality, route
cycling, or a general sign claim that adding metrics helps or hurts.

Policy or manuscript prose must not promote the toy beyond this finite-channel
contract unless a later theorem, simulation boundary, or application contract
adds the missing hypotheses and falsifiers.

## Retraction conditions

Retract or narrow any claim that:

- says reactive hardening converges while omitting separability, monotone
  capacity reduction, deterministic deficits, and fixed `V`;
- says adding metrics backfires while ignoring high-signal, low-harm,
  low-`kappa` additions;
- says static commitment dominates without a predeclared signal-adequacy
  floor;
- treats `kappa`, `h`, `gamma`, `w`, or `V` as available when they cannot be
  estimated, bounded, defended, or declared unavailable before observing the
  policy outcome.
