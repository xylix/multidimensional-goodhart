# Application response-modeling review synthesis

Status after consolidation: durable methodological synthesis. This file
replaces the separate hospital scorecard, application-template, and
application-promotion review files. Use it for application response-contract
discipline, not as current manuscript, empirical, theorem, or policy authority.
The live operational template is `research/applications/application_template.md`;
current claim licenses live in `research/claim_audits.md`.

## Consolidated verdict

The reusable result is a response-modeling contract. A serious application must
predeclare the type/action boundary, identify whether the mechanism changes
`W_theta`, `K_theta`, or both, declare proxy and hidden target variables,
state the effective aggregation rule, name a hidden harm/value model, specify
an evidence standard, list discriminator observations, and include
contract-failure conditions before interpreting score movement.

This is methodological discipline, not an empirical estimator. The application
reviews do not license patient-welfare estimates, research-value estimates,
policy optimality, theorem expansion, or generic anti-metric advice.

## Hospital and scientific-metrics transfer

The hospital scorecard review showed that the framework changes an audit
decision: do not credit readmission-score gains as patient-welfare gains until
composition, repeated fixed-hospital action traces, hidden patient outcomes,
and the effective aggregation rule have been checked. The review also narrowed
the claim: aggregate score movement does not identify welfare, harm, or the
selection/intervention channel.

The scientific-metrics transfer showed that the same contract fields can carry
across domains, but with weaker hidden-value observability. Scientific metrics
need researcher or lab panels, project traces, field normalization, delayed
uptake, qualitative review, and evidence about publication, citation, topic,
method, venue, grant, and open-science response channels. In many cases the
right output is to mark `kappa`, `h`, `gamma`, `w`, or `V` unavailable rather
than fill them in after observing outcomes.

## Reuse conditions

Application reuse is licensed only when the application:

1. Predeclares `U` and the type/action boundary.
2. Separates selection through `W_theta` from fixed-type response through
   `K_theta`.
3. Declares proxy, hidden target, aggregation, and hidden harm/value model.
4. Treats `kappa`, `h`, `gamma`, `w`, and `V` as declared, bounded, defended,
   or unavailable primitives.
5. Names observations stronger than aggregate score movement.
6. States how the application changes an audit, design, or evidence decision.
7. Includes a contract-failure condition that could actually fire.

Without these constraints, the template collapses into flexible redescription:
the analyst can call any post-hoc story a Goodhart mechanism after seeing which
score moved.

## Quadratic diagnostic boundary

The additive quadratic diagnostic

`m(d) = d^2 / (2 sum_j kappa_j w_j^2)`

and

`a_j^*(d) = d kappa_j w_j / sum_i kappa_i w_i^2`

survives only as a conditional audit heuristic. It can suggest which response
channels to inspect first under additive proxy gain, local separable quadratic
costs, declared `kappa`, and effective weights `w`. It does not predict real
behavior, estimate welfare, estimate research value, or prove policy
optimality.

## Promotion boundary

The promotion-ready claim is narrow: response-modeling contracts can make
institutional scorecard examples decision-relevant by forcing the writer to
declare channels, hidden targets, evidence, discriminators, and failure
conditions before interpreting score movement.

Footnote-level material only: finite-channel adaptive-hardening toys and the
quadratic diagnostic. Exclude theorem expansion, endogenous-stakes claims,
empirical welfare or research-value estimation, route-cycling theorems, broad
policy optimality, and universal anti-metric advice.
